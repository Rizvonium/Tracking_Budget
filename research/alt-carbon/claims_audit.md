# Alt Carbon claims audit (as of 1 Nov 2026)

Every figure comes from public sources. **C** = company-reported. Arithmetic is mine.

| Claim | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Land onboarded | 80,000 acres (C) | altcarbon.com/alt-labs; Microsoft PR, 11 Jun 2026 |
| Farmers | 35,000+ in 60+ gram panchayats (C) | Microsoft PR |

## Contradictions and gaps
1. **Acreage moves in both directions.** 60,000 acres "in six months" (Nov 2025); still "60,000+" (Apr 2026);
   80,000 (Jun 2026); ThePrint still reports ~60,000 (Jul 2026). The Nov 2025 target of
   100,000+ acres in 12 months has not been announced as met.
2. **Measured area is far below onboarded area.** The same Microsoft PR that claims 80,000 acres says samples
   cover "roughly twice the size of Manhattan", about 29,000 acres. The Aug 2025 blog says baseline samples
   came from ~30,000 acres.
3. **Credits imply a much smaller treated area.** At published field-trial rates (2.6–8.6 t CO2/ha/yr),
   9,567 t over ~1.5 years corresponds to ~1,800–6,000 acres. If all 80,000 acres contributed, the rate
   would be ~0.2 t/ha/yr, about 10x below published trials. Either way, "onboarded" ≠ treated and credited.
4. **Basalt tonnage is undisclosed.** Even at the claimed maximum ratio (1:4), 9,567 t CO2 needs ≥38,000 t basalt
   fully weathered. Realistic partial weathering implies far more. Only ~500 t (Oct 2024) and 1,200 t (2024)
   have been disclosed.
5. **The 1:4 ratio is the theoretical maximum, not a field result.** Basalt with ~10 % CaO and ~7 % MgO has a
   bicarbonate-pathway ceiling of ~0.31 t CO2/t. 1:4 = 0.25 t/t, about 80 % of that ceiling.
6. **Sample counts don't add up.** 15,000+ baseline samples (Aug 2025) plus 12,000 analysed in 8 weeks
   (early 2026) gives ≥27,000, yet the company reports "20,000+ to date" (Apr–Jun 2026). These may overlap,
   but that is unexplained.
7. **Lab capacity was revised down 6x.** Oct 2024: D-CAL "up to 600,000 samples/yr by 2025". 2026: 100,000/yr
   (later described as the combined capacity of both labs).
8. **Credit volume is far below forecasts.** Nov 2025: "capacity to issue 100,000 credits from 2026". June 2026:
   ~15,000 more expected by end-2026. The registry on 1 Nov 2026 shows 9,566.66 total and no issuance since May 2026.
9. **Yield claims grow without published data.** 15–20 % (Oct 2024) → 20–30 % tea and 30–50 % rice/bamboo
   (Nov 2024, under a year after the first application) → >30 % average → 30–40 % (Jan 2026).
   No trial data has been released.
10. **Permanence claim differs.** "10,000+ years" (website) vs "over 1,000 years" (Microsoft PR).
11. **MOL delivery doesn't match the registry.** 221 + 2,500 = 2,721 t delivered to MOL vs 2,986.72 issued on the
    MOL project.
12. **The 2030 targets are inconsistent.** 5 Mt is stated as cumulative in some sources and per year in others.
    Even the cumulative version needs ~520x the current issued volume in 4 years.
13. **Basic facts conflict.**
    - Pilot size: 300 vs 500 acres.
    - Family estate: 2,000 ha vs 1,000 acres.
    - Founded: Aug 2023 vs "late 2023" vs launched Jan 2024.
    - Lab capacity, credit volumes and farmer counts differ across releases.
14. **Omission.** The first Isometric project (v1.0, verifier Earthood) is "Validation unsuccessful" (2025).
    It is not mentioned in any company material.
15. **A metric that doesn't make sense.** "> 8 % local jobs created per 1000 tons CDR" (project page) mixes a
    percentage with a per-tonne rate.
16. **Farmer count implies estate workers.** 35,000 farmers on 80,000 acres is 2.3 acres each, yet much of the
    land is large estates. "Farmers" likely includes estate workers.
